Question18: How is ADSL speed measured?

Answer18: ADSL speed is measured in bits per second, specifically in Megabits per second (Mbps).

Data on the internet is measured in bits:

- 1000 bits = 1 kilobit
- 1000 kilobits = 1 megabit

The more bits your line can transfer per second, the faster your internet will be. We measure our ADSL speeds in Mbps (Megabits per second). For example, our ADSL packages range from up to 2 Mbps (Fast ADSL) to up to 40 Mbps (Elite Plus, where available).

Note that the advertised speed reflects the maximum sync speed between your location and our exchange, not the guaranteed real-world speed, as this can vary due to factors like distance from the exchange or network congestion.

You can check your actual connection speed anytime using our speed test tool.

Question19: What factors can affect the download rate of my DSL connection?

Answer19: Several factors can influence the download speed of your DSL connection. Here's what might be affecting your experience:

📌 **Key Factors Affecting DSL Speed:**

- Your PC performance - Older or slower devices may not achieve maximum speeds
- Access line quality - The physical condition and length of your copper line
- Local network capacity - How many users are sharing bandwidth in your area
- Network congestion - Especially during peak hours (7pm-9pm)
- Remote server performance - The speed of websites/services you're accessing
- Wi-Fi interference - Distance from router or electrical interference

⚠️ **Common Telkom-Specific Considerations:**

- You may have reached your SoftCap data limit (check via MY TELKOM account)
- Multiple users sharing your home connection simultaneously
- Distance from the phone exchange affects line quality

📌 **Quick Checks You Can Do:**

- Run a speed test (available on our website)
- Check for background updates/downloads on your devices
- Verify no one else is heavily using the connection
- Ensure your router is centrally located

For persistent issues, you might want to try our online Troubleshooter via the TELKOM GET HELP page.

Question37: What is 5G and What is Fibre ?

Answer37: Here's a clear explanation of 5G and Fibre technologies:

📌 **What is Fibre?**
Fibre is a bundle of fibre optic threads made of pure glass, each about the diameter of a human hair. It uses light instead of electricity to carry digital data signals, enabling virtually unlimited speed and capacity. Key features include:

- Superfast, stable broadband connection (e.g., downloads a 25GB HD video in ~34 minutes vs. 1.5 hours on VDSL)
- Unaffected by weather or distance from the exchange
- Ideal for households with multiple users streaming, gaming, or working simultaneously

📌 **What is 5G Broadband?**
5G is the latest generation of wireless mobile technology, offering:

- Faster download/upload speeds than 4G
- Enhanced performance for gaming, video streaming, and large app downloads
- High-speed connectivity without physical cables (unlike Fibre)

Both technologies deliver high-speed internet, with Fibre being wired and 5G being wireless. Fibre excels in reliability and consistent speeds, while 5G provides mobility and rapid deployment.
